How this works

All 40 core-game cards are preloaded with numbers and events. Quantities come from a community-maintained transcription (ZombiDeck / endead), cross-checked against the rulebook example, not from an official digital card list. If a number differs from your physical copy, tap that card in Deck to override it.

When an Abomination is spawned, the app also draws its type from your selected Abomination deck. Standard rules allow one on the board: a later Abomination spawn gives it an extra Activation. Optional Abomination Fest spawns an additional type after activating existing Abominations, until your chosen limit is reached. Mark a type as defeated when it leaves the board.

Rush: place the indicated zombies, then they immediately activate. Extra Activation: at Yellow, Orange or Red, all zombies of the listed type activate once more; Blue has no effect.

Official rules: Zombicide 2nd Edition rulebook. Unofficial personal-use fan helper, not endorsed by CMON or Guillotine Games.
