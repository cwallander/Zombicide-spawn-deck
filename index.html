How this works

The numbered core deck types and special card behavior are preloaded. Normal spawn counts must come from your physical cards or photos. This avoids inaccurate replacement-card data.

Rush: place the indicated zombies, then they immediately activate. Extra Activation: at Yellow, Orange or Red, all zombies of the listed type activate once more; Blue has no effect.
